# Plan Rack Power and Capacity

Hardware orders fail in the PDU, not the spreadsheet — budget watts, uplinks, and growth before the crate arrives.

## Why it matters

An 8-node payments edge that exceeds PDU budget ships into a brownout. Headroom is a first-class capacity signal.

2. Dual uplinks + growth plan
